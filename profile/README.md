## What we build

- **AI Strategy & Governance.** Strategy, AI policy, risk and governance frameworks, capability roadmaps. Senior judgment from people who actually build with the technology.
- **Custom Software & Apps.** Web platforms, internal tools, customer-facing products and MVPs, built around how your team actually works, in weeks, not quarters.
- **Reliable Automations.** Document processing, system integrations and structured workflows: deterministic, predictable and auditable.
- **Agentic AI.** Agents that reason across systems, handle exceptions and take action, for work that doesn't fit a fixed workflow.
- **3-D Digital Twins & Immersive Experiences.** Real-time 3-D environments that model your spaces, products or systems.
- **Real-Time Intelligence.** Live dashboards on your real systems and decision-grade visibility, from "what happened" to "what to do next."

## How we work

Advisors and doers, from boardroom to production.

- **Weeks, not months.** A working AI prototype in your environment in six weeks.
- **No spec required.** Requirements refined on screen, not on paper. We figure it out together.
- **Senior-led.** Senior judgment and senior delivery from the same team.
- **Partner, not vendor.** Ongoing care for the systems we ship, from the people who shipped them.

## What we stand for

- **Data sovereignty.** Your data stays yours. We design systems where you own and control the models, embeddings, and pipelines that touch it — not a vendor whose pricing or policies can change next quarter.
- **Privacy by default.** No surprise telemetry, no quiet retention, no third-party training on your inputs.
